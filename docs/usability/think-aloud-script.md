# Think-aloud session script (10 minutes)

For one participant at a time, for example a nurse or nursing assistant, using the app on a phone with **demo data only**. No real patient information is entered at any point.

**Before running it:** if the notes, recordings or SUS scores will be used in anything beyond the lab application (a report, a paper, a talk), ask the ODU IRB office for a written determination first (round 3 `permissions.md`, "Human subjects"). An informal check of the app with no recording and no plan to publish likely does not need review, but get that in writing.

**You need:** a phone with the app installed and demo data loaded (Patients & data, then "Load demo data"); this script; a notes sheet; the SUS form (`sus-form.md`); a timer. Do not record audio or video unless the participant agrees and the IRB question above is settled.

## 0:00 to 1:00 Introduction (read aloud)

> Thanks for helping. I'm testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers, and if something is confusing, that's exactly what I need to know. As you work, please say out loud what you're looking at, what you're trying to do, and what you expect to happen. If you go quiet, I'll remind you to keep talking. You can stop at any time. All the residents in the app are made up.

Practice (15 seconds): "Show me how you'd set an alarm on this phone, talking as you go."

## 1:00 to 8:00 Tasks

Read each task aloud and hand over the phone. Do not show where to tap. Note time on task, wrong taps, hesitations, and quotes.

| # | Task (read aloud) | Success looks like | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You're caring for P-DEMO-A. He just hit you during a transfer. Record it." | Correct resident selected; "Hitting" tapped once; notices the confirmation | 1 min |
| 2 | "Actually it happened a few minutes ago, during personal care. Add that if you can." | Opens "Add detail"; ticks "Time is approximate"; picks "Personal care or bathing" (the toast may close first: note it) | 1 min |
| 3 | "Oops, that was meant for P-DEMO-B. Fix it." | Uses Undo, or switches resident and finds no way to move the log (note what they try) | 1 min |
| 4 | "It's the end of your shift with P-DEMO-B and nothing happened today. Tell the app." | Finds "Nothing to log today" at the bottom of the Log screen | 1 min |
| 5 | "Is P-DEMO-A's screaming getting better or worse? How sure are you?" | Scores screen; reads row 13 and its arrow; mentions the change rule or unlogged days | 1.5 min |
| 6 | "The total went down. Does that mean his treatment is working?" | Reads the note that totals fall without treatment changes; names the behaviors that changed | 1 min |
| 7 | "Check the app's ratings for the last two weeks and change one you disagree with." | Day-14 review; changes one rating | 0.5 min |

Neutral prompts only: "What are you thinking?", "What did you expect to happen?", "Keep talking, please." If stuck past the limit: "Thanks, let's move on." Never say whether they got it right.

## 8:00 to 9:30 Questionnaire

Hand over the SUS form (`sus-form.md`). Ask for quick, first-impression answers.

## 9:30 to 10:00 Debrief

1. "What was the most frustrating part?"
2. "On a busy shift, would you actually use this? What would stop you?"
3. "Was there anything you expected to find and didn't?"

Thank them. Reload the demo data before the next session (Patients & data, "Load demo data").

## Notes sheet columns

participant code (no names) · task # · success (yes / partial / no) · time (s) · errors · quotes · observer note
